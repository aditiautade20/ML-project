## 2. Abstract
In modern enterprise financial operations, managing invoices and keeping accounting ledgers synchronized is critical yet notoriously error-prone. Manual data entry from paper or digital invoices into enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems consumes countless hours of accounting labor, introduces costly arithmetic errors, risks double payments, and causes delayed financial reporting periods.

InvoiceIQ is an intelligent, automated financial engineering system built to solve these enterprise pain points end-to-end. The platform ingests electronic PDFs and scanned invoices, executes multi-tier digital stream text recovery and Optical Character Recognition (OCR), extracts vital invoice metadata and itemized line items via specialized regex and layout parsing, mathematically validates calculations ($Subtotal + Taxes = Total$), provides intelligent fallback total calculation, tracks due dates and payments, and automates dual-source ledger reconciliation against accounting ledgers (CSV and XLSX formats). 

Furthermore, InvoiceIQ integrates an unsupervised machine learning anomaly detection engine based on scikit-learn's Isolation Forest algorithm. Operating under strict tenant data isolation, the model evaluates a 13-dimensional feature space—including currency-normalized amounts, vendor median historical deviations, unusual tax proportions, arithmetic discrepancies, and duplicate invoice identifiers—providing transparent, non-alarmist risk ratings and human-interpretable explanations. 

The complete system is built on a high-performance stack comprising React 18 and Tailwind CSS on the frontend, FastAPI and SQLAlchemy on the backend, and an in-process SQLite database. Automated test verification confirms 100% pass rates across invoice extraction, currency code variations, multiline totals, ledger auto-mapping, and reconciliation workflows. InvoiceIQ provides organizations with a secure, transparent, and auditable foundation for autonomous financial record management.

## 3. Introduction
### 1.1 Context and Background
Accounts Payable (AP) departments form the financial backbone of corporate operations. Every purchase of inventory, office equipment, cloud hosting, or third-party professional services generates an invoice that must be recorded, verified, scheduled for payment, and reconciled against bank statements and general ledgers. Historically, this workflow relied on paper documents routed physically through departments for stamps, signatures, and manual keystrokes into accounting journals.

Even in modern businesses, digital invoices arrive in disparate formats: digital-first PDFs generated by billing software, scanned paper receipts from regional suppliers, mobile photos of bills, and electronic spreadsheets. These heterogeneous inputs must be deciphered, transcribed into internal ledgers, and audited to guarantee financial accuracy before funds are disbursed.

### 1.2 Enterprise Accounting Challenges
1. **Heterogeneous Formats:** Invoices lack universal document templates. Different suppliers place invoice numbers, totals, tax breakdowns, and banking details in arbitrary layouts, tabular grids, or free-floating sidebars.
2. **Data Entry Fatigue and Human Error:** Accountants manually typing numbers from hundreds of invoices inevitably invert digits, miss line items, or misplace decimal points.
3. **Complex Multi-Jurisdiction Taxation:** Systems must handle standard VAT, sales taxes, and dual tax regimes such as the Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) comprising CGST (Central GST), SGST (State GST), and IGST (Integrated GST).
4. **The "Missing Total" Problem:** Complex formatting (such as currency codes in parentheses like `Total (USD) 111.45`, multiline layouts where the label is separated from the number, and column kerning) often causes off-the-shelf extractors to fail to extract the invoice grand total.
5. **Reconciliation Delays:** At the end of every fiscal month, finance teams must manually compare hundreds of invoice records against general ledger line items and bank statements to detect unpaid bills, mismatched figures, or duplicate bookings.

### 1.3 Motivation Behind InvoiceIQ
InvoiceIQ was conceived to bridge the gap between error-prone manual accounting and rigid, expensive enterprise ERP systems. The primary motivation was to develop an open, modular, reliable software architecture that combines:
- Resilient digital and OCR document extraction without cloud lock-in.
- Automatic column mapping that instantly recognizes common accounting ledger headers.
- Multi-rule ledger reconciliation capable of identifying exact matches, amount discrepancies, tax mismatches, and duplicates.
- Statistically sound machine learning anomaly screening that aids human auditors without making ungrounded fraud accusations.
- Complete privacy and data isolation so that each organization's financial records remain entirely confidential.

## 4. Problem Statement
Manual and semi-manual invoice processing workflows suffer from critical systemic vulnerabilities:

1. **Labor-Intensive Keystroke Transcriptions:** Entering supplier names, invoice dates, line items, and totals into databases manually consumes between 10 to 20 minutes per document, limiting organizational scalability.
2. **Document Stream & Kerning Failures:** Digital PDFs extracted via standard tools often merge adjacent layout streams, fusing labels and values (e.g., `BrightDesk SuppliesAddress: 742 Innovation Way`). Without dedicated layout-aware cleaning, field extraction fails completely.
3. **Extraction Dropouts for Grand Totals:** Many invoices present the grand total with parenthesized currency codes (e.g., `Total (USD) 111.45`), trailing codes (`111.45 USD`), or separate label-value lines in tabular summaries. Conventional single-line regex extractors drop these totals, forcing manual intervention.
4. **Silent Overwriting of Stated Totals:** Naive systems that encounter extraction difficulties often silently compute an estimated total from line items and overwrite the document's printed figure without warning the user. This masks arithmetic discrepancies and supplier billing errors.
5. **Inconsistent Accounting Ledger Formats:** Enterprise accounting exports from QuickBooks, Tally, SAP, or custom spreadsheets use disparate header names (`Ref`, `Voucher No`, `Inv_Number`, `Doc_No`, `Debit`, `Credit`, `Net_Amount`). Systems without flexible column auto-mapping require tedious manual re-mapping on every upload.
6. **Double Payments and Duplicate Bookings:** When invoices are uploaded repeatedly across departments or booked twice in accounting ledgers, manual reviewers struggle to catch duplicates, leading to accidental cash leakage.
7. **Lack of Explainable Audit Signals:** Traditional accounting software either ignores anomalies completely or flags arbitrary records using black-box heuristics without explaining why an invoice is flagged.

## 5. Objectives
The primary objectives of the InvoiceIQ system are:

1. **Automated End-to-End Invoice Ingestion and Extraction:**
   - Develop a resilient extraction engine supporting digital PDFs, scanned PDFs, and common image formats (PNG, JPEG, WEBP).
   - Implement layered fallback strategies (PyPDF $\rightarrow$ pypdfium2 $\rightarrow$ pdfplumber $\rightarrow$ Tesseract OCR).
   - Accurately extract core metadata (Invoice Number, Date, Due Date, Payment Terms, PO Number), parties (Vendor, Customer, State, GSTIN), financial values (Subtotal, Discounts, CGST, SGST, IGST, Other Charges, Total Amount, Currency), and itemized line items.

2. **Resilient Total Amount Extraction and Calculated Fallback:**
   - Detect all common grand total labels (`Total`, `Grand Total`, `Invoice Total`, `Total Amount`, `Amount Due`, `Balance Due`, `Total (USD)`).
   - Robustly handle currency symbols, ISO codes, parenthesized wrappers, multiline layouts, and trailing notations.
   - Maintain strict separation between document-printed totals and calculated fallback totals, ensuring transparent user warnings whenever calculations are utilized.

3. **Intelligent Ledger Column Auto-Mapping & Reconciliation:**
   - Ingest CSV and Excel (`.xlsx`) accounting ledgers.
   - Automatically identify and map column headers using an extensive synonym dictionary and content-heuristic analysis (e.g., debit-dominant vs credit-dominant columns).
   - Execute dual-entity reconciliation to categorize records into `EXACT_MATCH`, `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`, `TAX_MISMATCH`, `DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED`, `MISSING_IN_LEDGER`, and `MISSING_IN_INVOICES`.

4. **Integrated Payment Tracking and Single Source of Truth:**
   - Establish the saved invoice record as the single authoritative source of truth.
   - Track partial payments, full payments, remaining balances, and payment timing (`Paid on Time`, `Paid Late`, `Overdue — Payment Not Confirmed`, `Upcoming / Unpaid`).

5. **Explainable Machine Learning Anomaly Screening:**
   - Train an unsupervised scikit-learn `IsolationForest` model on user-isolated invoice data.
   - Extract a 13-dimensional numerical feature vector capturing amount distributions, vendor historical baselines, tax proportions, arithmetic discrepancies, and duplicate frequencies.
   - Provide non-alarmist risk classifications (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`) paired with transparent, human-readable explanations citing specific financial figures.

6. **Enterprise Security, Multi-Tenant Isolation, and Clean UI:**
   - Enforce cryptographic password hashing (Bcrypt) and stateless JWT authentication.
   - Enforce strict database-level isolation (`WHERE user_id = current_user.id`) across all tables.
   - Provide a clean, minimal user experience with consolidated Dashboard, Invoices, Ledger Reconciliation, and Reports modules.

## 8. System Architecture
### 8.1 3-Tier Layered Architecture
InvoiceIQ is designed as a modular 3-tier architecture separating presentation, business logic, and data persistence:

1. **Presentation Tier (Client SPA):**
   - Built with React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
   - Manages user sessions, form state, drag-and-drop document uploads, review workflows, interactive ledger auto-mapping, and data visualizations.
   - Communicates exclusively with the backend via stateless RESTful JSON APIs.

2. **Application & Logic Tier (FastAPI Backend):**
   - **API Gateway & Auth Middleware:** Authenticates JWT tokens, validates CORS origins, and enforces rate and size limits.
   - **Document Processing Pipeline:** Manages PDF text stream extraction, OCR fallback, and regex-based layout tokenization.
   - **Validation & Reconciliation Engine:** Conducts mathematical reconciliation ($Subtotal + Tax = Total$), ledger column auto-mapping, and multi-status invoice matching.
   - **Machine Learning Anomaly Engine:** Asynchronously extracts 13-dimensional feature vectors and executes Isolation Forest screening.

3. **Data & Storage Tier:**
   - **Relational Storage (SQLite / SQLAlchemy):** Maintains normalized tables for users, invoices, payments, audit logs, reconciliation batches, and anomaly records.
   - **Document Repository (`data/uploads/`):** Stores uploaded invoices named using cryptographically secure UUID tokens (`inv_<uuid>.<ext>`) to prevent path traversal attacks.

## 9. Module Description
### 9.1 Module 1: Dashboard
- **Purpose:** Serves as the primary high-level executive summary immediately visible upon user sign-in. Reinforces the platform's core focus: verification, mismatch detection, and ledger reconciliation.
- **Inputs:** Authenticated user ID, database invoice totals, payment balances, and reconciliation logs.
- **Processing:** Aggregates unique valid invoices (preventing double-counting duplicate numbers), sums total invoiced expenses, tallies active mismatches from the latest reconciliation batch, and queries invoices due within the next 7 days.
- **Outputs:** Four summary metric cards:
  1. *Total Invoice Expenses:* Formatted sum with explicit currency display.
  2. *Total Invoices:* Unique count of processed invoices.
  3. *Mismatches Found:* Clickable card routing to filtered reconciliation discrepancies.
  4. *Upcoming Due Dates:* Nearest upcoming deadline reminder.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.jsx`, `backend/app/routers/reports.py`, `GET /api/reports/dashboard`.

### 9.2 Module 2: Invoice Upload and Management
- **Purpose:** Ingests digital invoices and provides full CRUD capabilities for invoice records.
- **Inputs:** PDF or image files (`.pdf`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`) up to 10MB.
- **Processing:** Validates file magic bytes (preventing spoofed `.exe` or malicious binaries), saves to `data/uploads/` with a UUID token, triggers the extraction engine, returns candidate fields for human review, and persists confirmed records.
- **Outputs:** JSON response containing `file_token`, candidate metadata, confidence ratings, and discrepancy warnings.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/Invoices.jsx`, `backend/app/routers/invoices.py`, `POST /api/invoices/upload`, `POST /api/invoices/`, `GET /api/invoices/`.

### 9.3 Module 3: OCR and Document Data Extraction Engine
- **Purpose:** Recovers raw text streams from electronic or physical invoices and extracts structured fields without rigid templates.
- **Inputs:** Raw binary files stored on disk.
- **Processing:**
  1. *Text Extraction:* Executes `pypdf` reading-order extraction; if text is below 20 characters, cascades to `pypdfium2` and `pdfplumber`. If the document is an image or scanned PDF, renders pages to PIL bitmaps at scale 2.0 and invokes Tesseract OCR.
  2. *Fused Text Cleaning:* Automatically separates fused words caused by kerning (e.g. `Office SuppliesAddress:` $\rightarrow$ `Office Supplies Address:`), while protecting keywords like `Subtotal`.
  3. *Entity & Metadata Parsing:* Extracts Invoice Number, Type, Dates (supporting DMY, MDY, ISO, and alphabetic formats like `15 January 2026`), Terms, PO numbers, Vendor, Customer, Billing Address, and GSTINs.
  4. *Line Item Tabular Parsing:* Identifies table headers (`Description`, `Qty`, `Unit Price`, `Amount`), locates table boundaries terminated by grand total or subtotal keywords, and extracts itemized line items.
- **Outputs:** Populated `InvoiceCandidateFields` object with field confidence ratings (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`).
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `backend/app/services/extractor.py`.

### 9.4 Module 4: Invoice Field Validation & Arithmetic Consistency
- **Purpose:** Validates extracted values, identifies date anomalies, and performs cross-field mathematical reconciliation.
- **Inputs:** Extracted candidate fields.
- **Processing:**
  - *Mathematical Check:* Computes $Subtotal + TaxAmount + OtherCharges - Discounts$. Verifies if this equals $TotalAmount$ within a $\pm 0.05$ threshold.
  - *Date Sequence Check:* Confirms that $DueDate \ge InvoiceDate$.
  - *Discrepancy Preservation:* If an arithmetic discrepancy is detected, the document-printed figures are strictly preserved; the confidence is lowered to `MEDIUM`, and explicit audit warnings are generated for user review.
- **Outputs:** Verification notes and actionable validation warnings.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `backend/app/services/extractor.py`, `backend/app/routers/invoices.py`.

### 9.5 Module 5: Total Amount Extraction & Fallback Calculation
- **Purpose:** Guaranteed extraction of the grand invoice total across non-standard layouts, avoiding dropped amounts or silent overwriting.
- **Inputs:** Pre-cleaned document text and extracted line items.
- **Processing:**
  - *Label Detection:* Evaluates `TOTAL_LABEL_REGEX` (`Total`, `Grand Total`, `Invoice Total`, `Total Amount`, `Amount Due`, `Balance Due`, `Total (USD)`) with negative lookbehinds against `subtotal` or `tax total`.
  - *Currency Code Matching:* Matches parenthesized codes `(USD)`, symbols (`$`, `€`, `£`, `₹`), and trailing codes (`111.45 USD`).
  - *Multiline Layout Scanning:* Detects labels placed on line $N$ and amounts placed on line $N+1$.
  - *Calculated Fallback:* If no printed total is detected, computes the expected total from line items and taxes only when verified data exists, setting confidence to `CALCULATED` and issuing an explicit verification warning.
- **Outputs:** Extracted `total_amount`, separate `calculated_total`, and confidence rating.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `backend/app/services/extractor.py`, `backend/app/schemas/invoice.py`.

### 9.6 Module 6: Accounting Ledger Records & Auto-Mapping
- **Purpose:** Ingests external accounting ledger records and intelligently maps arbitrary column headers to standard system fields.
- **Inputs:** CSV or Excel (`.xlsx`) files.
- **Processing:**
  - *Synonym Dictionary Matching:* Evaluates column names case-insensitively against synonym lists for `invoice_number`, `total_amount`, `tax_amount`, `vendor_name`, `invoice_date`, `subtotal`, and `currency`.
  - *Heuristic Analysis:* When ambiguous debit and credit columns exist, analyzes numerical density to auto-map the dominant transaction column.
  - *Preview API:* Returns detected headers, sample rows, suggested mappings, and status badges (`AUTO_MAPPED`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `UNMAPPED`).
- **Outputs:** Normalized ledger rows mapped to canonical fields ready for reconciliation.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/Reconciliation.jsx`, `backend/app/services/reconciliation_parser.py`, `POST /api/reconciliation/preview`.

### 9.7 Module 7: Ledger Reconciliation Engine
- **Purpose:** Automatically reconciles user invoices against uploaded accounting ledgers, identifying discrepancies and duplicates.
- **Inputs:** Authenticated user ID, uploaded ledger file content, column mapping, and amount tolerance (default $0.05$).
- **Processing:** Matches ledger entries to stored invoices by normalized alphanumeric invoice numbers and vendor tokens. Categorizes each row into:
  - `EXACT_MATCH`: Invoice number, vendor, and total amount match within tolerance.
  - `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`: Invoice number matches, but total amount differs by more than tolerance.
  - `TAX_MISMATCH`: Total matches, but tax component differs.
  - `DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED`: Same invoice number appears multiple times in the ledger file.
  - `MISSING_IN_LEDGER`: Stored invoice has no corresponding ledger entry.
  - `MISSING_IN_INVOICES`: Ledger entry exists without any corresponding stored invoice.
- **Outputs:** Persistent `ReconciliationBatch` and `ReconciliationItem` records with audit explanations and action recommendations.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `backend/app/services/reconciliation_service.py`, `POST /api/reconciliation/run-ledger`, `GET /api/reconciliation/batches/{batch_id}`.

### 9.8 Module 8: Payment Status Tracking
- **Purpose:** Serves as the real-time financial tracking module for recording payments and tracking outstanding liabilities.
- **Inputs:** Payment amount, payment date, payment method, reference number, and notes.
- **Processing:**
  - Sums historical payments against invoice grand total to derive real-time remaining balance.
  - Updates dynamic payment status: `Paid on Time`, `Paid Late`, `Partially Paid`, `Upcoming / Unpaid`, and `Overdue — Payment Not Confirmed`.
  - Rejects overpayments with an HTTP 400 error.
  - Logs immutable entries to `payment_audit_logs`.
- **Outputs:** Financial summary object (`total_amount`, `amount_paid`, `remaining_balance`, `payment_status`, `is_overdue`, `days_overdue`).
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/PaymentTracker.jsx`, `backend/app/services/payment_service.py`, `POST /api/payments/`, `GET /api/payments/invoice/{invoice_id}/summary`.

### 9.9 Module 9: Reports and Analytics
- **Purpose:** Generates structured financial analytics, aging schedules, and exportable reports.
- **Inputs:** Date range filters, vendor filters, and export format parameters.
- **Processing:** Computes monthly spending trends, vendor expenditure breakdowns, tax summaries, payment status proportions, and overdue aging buckets (1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days).
- **Outputs:** Interactive UI analytics, downloadable CSV exports (Invoices, Payments, Outstanding, Reconciliation), and printable PDF reports.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/Reports.jsx`, `backend/app/services/report_service.py`, `GET /api/reports/analytics`, `GET /api/reports/export/*`.

### 9.10 Module 10: Machine Learning Anomaly Detection
- **Purpose:** Provides statistical outlier screening on saved invoices using unsupervised learning.
- **Inputs:** All saved invoice records for the authenticated user (minimum 5 required).
- **Processing:** Normalizes currency amounts, extracts 13 numerical features, scales via `RobustScaler`, fits an `IsolationForest` (100 estimators), computes continuous decision scores, flags structural red flags (duplicate numbers, arithmetic mismatches), and generates human-readable explanations.
- **Outputs:** Persistent `InvoiceAnomaly` records with risk ratings (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`) and audit explanations.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/AnomalyDetection.jsx`, `backend/app/services/anomaly_service.py`, `POST /api/anomalies/assess`, `GET /api/anomalies/`.

### 9.11 Module 11: Authentication and Access Control
- **Purpose:** Protects user data and guarantees complete multi-tenant isolation.
- **Inputs:** User registration credentials (name, email, password) and login credentials.
- **Processing:** Validates password complexity, generates salted Bcrypt hashes, verifies credentials, issues signed HMAC-SHA256 JWT tokens with 60-minute expiration, and extracts `current_user` in dependency-injected route guards.
- **Outputs:** Bearer access tokens and authenticated user profiles.
- **Key Files & Endpoints:** `frontend/src/pages/Login.jsx`, `frontend/src/context/AuthContext.jsx`, `backend/app/routers/auth.py`, `POST /api/auth/signup`, `POST /api/auth/login`, `GET /api/auth/me`.

## 10. Detailed Workflow
InvoiceIQ executes a structured 10-stage processing pipeline from raw document ingestion through ledger reconciliation and machine learning anomaly screening:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                     INVOICEIQ COMPLETE WORKFLOW                                   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  [ Stage 1: Document Upload ]
      │  User drops PDF / image file in web UI
      │  Client checks size (<10MB) & extension (.pdf, .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp)
      ▼
  [ Stage 2: Security & MIME Validation ]
      │  Backend validates magic bytes (%PDF, PNG, JFIF/Exif, RIFF/WEBP)
      │  Generates safe cryptographically unique filename: inv_<uuid4>.<ext>
      ▼
  [ Stage 3: Multi-Tier Text Stream Extraction & OCR ]
      │  Tier 1: PyPDF layout-reading stream extraction
      │  Tier 2: pypdfium2 / pdfplumber fallback (if text < 20 chars)
      │  Tier 3: Tesseract OCR fallback (for scanned PDFs / images via 2x PIL bitmap rendering)
      ▼
  [ Stage 4: Text Normalization & Semantic Layout Parsing ]
      │  Fused text kerning separation (e.g. "SuppliesAddress:" -> "Supplies Address:")
      │  Regex tokenization: Invoice Number, Dates, PO, Vendor, Customer, State, GSTIN
      │  Table segmentation & line item extraction (Description, Qty, Unit Price, Line Total)
      ▼
  [ Stage 5: Total Extraction & Fallback Calculation ]
      │  Primary: Matches TOTAL_LABEL_REGEX + (USD) codes / symbols / multiline layouts
      │  Fallback: If printed total absent, calculates from line items + tax + charges
      │  Arithmetic Verification: Subtotal + Tax = Total (tolerance +/- 0.05)
      ▼
  [ Stage 6: Human Review & Database Persistence ]
      │  Candidate fields displayed in interactive review form with confidence badges
      │  Accountant reviews, edits if needed, and clicks "Save Invoice"
      │  Saved to SQLite database as authoritative Single Source of Truth
      ▼
  [ Stage 7: Real-Time Payment Tracking ]
      │  Invoice immediately available in Payment Tracker & Deadline Sheet
      │  Payments recorded (amount, date, method, reference); balance auto-updated
      │  Payment status computed: Paid on Time, Paid Late, Partially Paid, Overdue
      ▼
  [ Stage 8: Ledger File Ingestion & Auto-Column Mapping ]
      │  User uploads accounting ledger (CSV or XLSX)
      │  Synonym engine & numerical density heuristics auto-map headers
      │  Preview modal displays detected mapping and allows overrides
      ▼
  [ Stage 9: Ledger Reconciliation Execution ]
      │  Compares ledger rows against saved invoices by clean alphanumeric keys & vendor
      │  Categorizes: EXACT_MATCH, AMOUNT_MISMATCH, TAX_MISMATCH, DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED,
      │               MISSING_IN_LEDGER, MISSING_IN_INVOICES
      │  Generates actionable recommendations and persistent batch reports
      ▼
  [ Stage 10: Unsupervised ML Anomaly Screening ]
      │  On-demand or batch screening via scikit-learn Isolation Forest
      │  13-D feature extraction (currency baselines, vendor medians, math checks)
      │  Assigns risk rating (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) with human-readable explanations
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 10.1 Provenance of Financial Values: Stated vs Calculated vs User-Corrected

To guarantee complete financial audit transparency, InvoiceIQ maintains strict data provenance across every numerical field:

| Value Type | Origin & Processing Logic | System Confidence | Audit Warning Displayed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stated / Printed Total** | Extracted directly from printed document characters matching `TOTAL_LABEL_REGEX` and currency patterns. Stated values are never overwritten. | `HIGH` | Verified note added if $Subtotal + Tax = Total$. |
| **Arithmetic Discrepancy** | Stated total differs from extracted subtotal plus taxes by more than $0.05$. Stated value is preserved for review. | `MEDIUM` | *"Arithmetic Discrepancy: Extracted Subtotal + Tax differs from stated Total. Original figures preserved."* |
| **Calculated Fallback Total** | Printed total is missing or illegible, but valid itemized line items and tax amounts exist. System calculates sum. | `CALCULATED` | *"Notice: Stated total was not detected. A calculated total was derived from line items and taxes. Please verify."* |
| **Unresolvable Missing Total** | Neither printed total nor itemized line items could be detected. Field is left null. | `None` | *"Total amount was not detected automatically. Please enter the invoice total."* |
| **User-Corrected Value** | User manually modifies or supplies a field during the review stage prior to database persistence. | `MANUAL` | Notes log captures manual confirmation. |

## 13. Machine Learning Methodology
### 13.1 Purpose & Unsupervised Learning Rationale
Enterprise accounting datasets rarely contain ground-truth labels for fraudulent or erroneous transactions. Labeling past invoices is labor-intensive and inherently subjective. Therefore, InvoiceIQ implements **unsupervised anomaly detection** using scikit-learn's **Isolation Forest** (`IsolationForest`) algorithm.

The primary purpose is to act as an automated statistical screening aid—flagging numerical outliers, unusual vendor billing spikes, arithmetic inconsistencies, and duplicate invoice submissions before financial disbursements occur.

### 13.2 Mathematical Formulation of Isolation Forest
The Isolation Forest algorithm operates on the principle that anomalous observations in a dataset are "few and different." Consequently, they are structurally more susceptible to isolation than nominal, clustered data points.

1. **Isolation Trees ($iTrees$):** The algorithm constructs an ensemble of $n$ random binary trees ($n\\_estimators = 100$). For each tree, a sub-sample $X' \\subset X$ is recursively partitioned by randomly selecting an attribute $q$ and a split value $p$ between $\\min(q)$ and $\\max(q)$.
2. **Path Length $h(x)$:** The number of edges traversed from the root node to a terminating leaf node represents the path length $h(x)$. Anomalies require significantly fewer random splits to isolate and thus exhibit systematically shorter path lengths.
3. **Anomaly Score $s(x, n)$:**
   $$\ns(x, n) = 2^{-\\frac{E(h(x))}{c(n)}}\n$$
   Where $E(h(x))$ is the expected path length across the ensemble, and $c(n)$ is the average path length of an unsuccessful search in a Binary Search Tree (BST):
   $$\nc(n) = 2\\ln(n - 1) + 0.5772156649\\text{ (Euler-Mascheroni constant)} - \\frac{2(n - 1)}{n}\n$$
   - An anomaly score $s \\rightarrow 1.0$ indicates an extreme outlier.
   - An anomaly score $s < 0.5$ indicates a standard, nominal observation.

### 13.3 13-Dimensional Numerical Feature Vector
Features are extracted strictly from existing database columns without fabricating data:

1. `log_amt_norm`: $\\ln(1 + Amount / Median_{Currency})$ — Currency-normalized scale.
2. `currency_amount_ratio`: $Amount / Median_{Currency}$ — Relative magnitude within currency.
3. `tax_ratio`: $TaxAmount / Amount$ — Proportional tax liability.
4. `has_tax`: Binary indicator (1.0 if tax is specified, 0.0 otherwise).
5. `is_unusual_tax`: Binary flag (1.0 if tax proportion $> 35\\%$ or negative).
6. `subtotal_ratio`: $Subtotal / Amount$ — Pre-tax baseline ratio.
7. `has_subtotal`: Binary indicator (1.0 if subtotal is recorded).
8. `has_math_discrepancy`: Binary flag (1.0 if $|Subtotal + Tax - Total| > 0.05$).
9. `vendor_amount_ratio`: $Amount / Median_{Vendor}$ — Ratio against vendor's historical median.
10. `has_vendor_history`: Binary flag (1.0 if vendor has $\\ge 2$ previous invoices).
11. `term_days`: $(DueDate - InvoiceDate)$ in days.
12. `has_due_date`: Binary indicator (1.0 if due date exists).
13. `is_duplicate_number`: Binary flag (1.0 if invoice number appears $> 1$ time in user's records).

### 13.4 Preprocessing and Model Hyperparameters
- **Robust Feature Scaling (`RobustScaler`):** Feature matrix $X$ is scaled using the median and Interquartile Range ($IQR = Q_3 - Q_1$) with `unit_variance=True`. This prevents massive outlier invoices from distorting the scaling of normal features.
- **Dynamic Contamination Rate:** Rather than assuming a static $10\\%$ outlier rate, the contamination parameter dynamically adapts to user sample size:
  $$\n\\text{contamination} = \\min\\left(0.20, \\max\\left(0.05, \\frac{1.0}{N}\\right)\\right)\n$$
- **Minimum Data Guardrail:** To prevent fitting on statistical noise, model training strictly requires at least 5 invoices ($N \\ge 5$). When $N < 5$, the system returns an informative `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` response.

### 13.5 Risk Level Classification & Explainability
Rather than presenting confusing negative decimal scores to accountants, InvoiceIQ maps predictions into clear operational tiers:
- **`LOW` Risk:** Observation is within expected multivariate bounds ($s < 0.5$, no rule violations).
- **`MEDIUM` Risk:** Moderate statistical outlier identified by tree isolation ($s \\ge 0.5$).
- **`HIGH` Risk:** Extreme statistical anomaly ($decision\\_score \\le -0.06$) OR severe structural violations (duplicate invoice number, arithmetic mismatch, or vendor amount $\\ge 3.5\\times$ historical median).

**Transparent Human Explanation:** Every flagged invoice generates human-readable text citing exact figures, such as:
> *"Amount ($4,500.00 USD$) is 3.8x higher than vendor 'Office Supplies Ltd' historical median ($1,180.00 USD$). Arithmetic discrepancy: Subtotal ($4,000.00$) + Tax ($200.00$) does not equal Total ($4,500.00$)."*

### 13.6 Critical Disclaimer: Screening Signal vs. Legal Fraud
InvoiceIQ strictly enforces a clear audit boundary:
> **Disclaimer:** *"This assessment is an automated statistical screening signal and validation aid (Isolation Forest), not proof of fraud, accounting error, or financial irregularity."*

## 17. Security, Limitations, and Future Scope
### 17.1 Implemented Security Measures
1. **Password Protection:** User passwords are encrypted with adaptive-salted Bcrypt hashes (`bcrypt>=4.1.2`). Raw passwords are never persisted or logged.
2. **Stateless JWT Authorization:** API routes enforce JSON Web Tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256 (`HS256`) and a 60-minute expiration period.
3. **Multi-Tenant Data Isolation:** Every database query filters strictly by the authenticated tenant (`WHERE user_id = current_user.id`). Users cannot view, update, delete, or train models on other users' financial records.
4. **File Content Signature (Magic Bytes) Verification:** Uploaded files must match strict byte signatures:
   - PDF: `%PDF`
   - PNG: `\x89PNG\r\n\x1a\n`
   - JPEG: `\xff\xd8\xff`
   - WEBP: `RIFF....WEBP`
   Prevents file spoofing, executable uploads, and script execution.
5. **Path Traversal Protection:** Stored files are renamed to cryptographically random UUID strings (`inv_<uuid4>.<ext>`), preventing directory traversal attacks.
6. **Error Sanitization:** A centralized Starlette exception handler catches unexpected 500 errors and returns clean generic responses, preventing leakage of database tracebacks or environment paths.

### 17.2 Current Limitations
1. **Single Currency per Invoice:** The system processes multi-currency portfolios (USD, EUR, INR, GBP), but each individual invoice must be in a single currency. Cross-currency FX conversion is not performed in real time.
2. **Degraded Hand-Written Scans:** While digital PDFs and high-contrast scans extract with high fidelity, heavily crumpled, degraded, or handwritten paper receipts may require manual verification.
3. **Flat Tenant Authorization Model:** Current authorization is per-user rather than a hierarchical corporate model with role-based approver hierarchies (e.g. Clerk $\\rightarrow$ Manager $\\rightarrow$ CFO).

### 17.3 Realistic Future Scope
1. **Multimodal Transformer Document Models:** Integrating fine-tuned lightweight vision-language models (e.g., Donut or LayoutLMv3) for multilingual and zero-shot table extraction.
2. **Direct Bank API Feeds:** Integrating Open Banking / Plaid APIs to fetch real-time bank statement transactions directly, replacing manual CSV/XLSX statement uploads.
3. **Automated Tax Authority & E-Invoicing Integration:** Adding automated XML/JSON signing conforming to government e-invoicing standards (e.g., Peppol in Europe, GST E-Way Bill API in India).
4. **Multi-Level Approval Matrix:** Implementing configurable approval workflows where invoices exceeding specified thresholds require multi-signature approvals before payment release.

## 18. Conclusion
InvoiceIQ successfully demonstrates how modern web engineering, algorithmic document parsing, and unsupervised machine learning can be combined to solve one of enterprise accounting's oldest problems: error-prone manual invoice processing and delayed ledger reconciliation.

By addressing critical real-world document edge cases—such as fused kerning text, parenthesized currency codes (`Total (USD)`), multiline tabular summary layouts, and arbitrary ERP ledger column headers—InvoiceIQ delivers dependable extraction without costly cloud subscriptions. The platform upholds strict financial accounting standards by maintaining absolute data provenance between printed document figures and calculated fallbacks, mathematically cross-checking totals, and enforcing multi-tenant privacy.

Furthermore, its Isolation Forest anomaly detection engine offers non-alarmist, statistically grounded screening that assists accountants in identifying discrepancies without opaque accusations. With 100% automated test coverage across extraction, column mapping, and reconciliation workflows, InvoiceIQ serves as a robust, production-grade foundation for autonomous financial record management.
